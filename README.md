# AI Career Navigator for Career Changers

Building AI course project

## Summary

AI Career Navigator is an intelligent career guidance platform designed to help adults transition into new careers. The system analyzes a user's skills, experience, interests, and learning goals to recommend job opportunities, training programs, certifications, and personalized career paths. The project is inspired by my own experience exploring new career opportunities while learning artificial intelligence.

## Background

Many people want to change careers or improve their professional prospects but struggle to understand which skills employers are looking for and what steps they should take next. My personal motivation comes from my own journey of learning AI and exploring new employment opportunities.

## How is it used?

Users provide information about their experience, education, certifications, skills, career interests, and preferred work location. The AI analyzes strengths, identifies skill gaps, recommends jobs and training, and offers resume suggestions.

## Data Sources and AI Methods

Public job listings, occupational databases, learning platforms, and user resumes. Methods include NLP, classification, recommendation systems, and similarity analysis.

## Challenges

AI cannot guarantee employment. Recommendations may be affected by incomplete data, labor market changes, or user-specific circumstances. Privacy and bias must be considered.

## What Next?

Future improvements include interview coaching, salary forecasting, cover letter generation, networking recommendations, and labor market trend analysis.
